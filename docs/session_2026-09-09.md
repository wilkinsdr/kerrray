## 2  Findings

- Existing Euler/RK4/RK45 propagators mis-trace rays emitted near a radial turning point that initially head towards the polar axis (spurious radial sign flip); ~4% of lamppost rays at `θ = 10⁻³`, landing radius off by ~70%. Confirmed against direct Carter quadratures and an independent scipy integration.
- RK45 stalls at `RK45_STEPLIM` on plunging rays (252/5040 lamppost rays; 65/2500 image-plane rays) and never terminates on the `x = y = 0` image-plane ray (NaN constants of motion).
- Lamppost benchmark (4 threads): symplectic order 4 at `h₀ = 0.01`, `max_tstep = 0.25` is 2.5× faster than RK45 at `10⁻⁸` with no diverged rays; for image planes started at `dist = 1000` the far field dominates (~4000 steps at `max_tstep = 0.25`) and the symplectic run is ~2× slower than RK45 — the far-field step policy is the open question for the work–precision study (T3).

## 4  Work–precision sweep (T3) — added later the same day

`src/tests/integrator_sweep.cpp` (+ `python/integrator_sweep_plot.py`; results in `dat/integrator_sweep_{lamppost,imageplane}.{csv,png}`). Reference: symplectic order 6, `h₀ = 0.001`, `max_tstep = 0.05`, validated against direct Carter quadratures with full-precision constants (`|dr| ≤ 2×10⁻⁷`, `|dt| ≤ 6×10⁻⁷` on four rays of different types).

- Euler, RK4 and RK45 all sit on accuracy floors that their precision/tolerance knobs cannot move (`MIN_STEP`, `MAXDT`, `MAXDPHI`, the linear disc-landing clamp): lamppost RK4 median `|dr| = 2×10⁻⁴` and 9% of rays diverged at every precision; RK45 ~5400 steps/ray and median `|dr| ≈ 6×10⁻⁵` at every tolerance.
- Symplectic: the far-field `max_tstep` cap sets the accuracy on image planes (error ∝ `max_tstep⁴`), `h₀` sets it in the strong field; the growth policy (`max_tstep ∝ r` beyond `maxtstep_rlim = 100`) costs nothing on the lamppost set and ~3× in error on the image plane while cutting steps 3×.
- Recommended defaults, now set: order 6, `max_tstep = 1` with growth, `h₀ = 1/precision = 0.01`. Lamppost: 150 steps/ray, 0.21 s (vs RK45 1.4 s), 90% `|dg| = 1.2×10⁻¹⁰` (vs RK45 best `2.4×10⁻⁵`). Image plane (`dist = 1000`): 347 steps/ray, 0.20 s (vs RK45 0.37 s), 90% `|dg| = 1.7×10⁻⁸` (vs RK45 best `1.6×10⁻⁵`).

## 5  Photon-ring stress test (T4)

`src/tests/photon_ring_test.cpp`: 140 rays with constants of motion offset by `ε = ±10⁻²…10⁻⁸` from the Bardeen critical curve at `r_ph ∈ {1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5}`, `a = 0.998`, started at `r = 1000`, 60°, both polar directions, traced with no polar stopping condition until escape (`r = 1500`) or capture (up to 13 equatorial crossings).

- Chaos floor: photon-sphere orbits amplify errors by ~e⁶ per orbit, so two fine symplectic references at slightly different steps already disagree on fate/winding number for `ε ≤ 10⁻⁴` (18–19/20 fate, 9–18/20 crossings). Agreement rates at small `ε` must be read against that row.
- Symplectic default (o6, `h₀ = 0.01`, `max_tstep = 1`): 1047 steps/ray, 0.03 s for all 140 rays, fate and winding number 100% correct for `ε ≥ 10⁻³`, exit-direction error `1.6×10⁻⁴` (median) at `ε = 10⁻²`, and at the chaos floor for smaller `ε`; no step-limit failures; `|H_M| ≤ 1.3×10⁻⁷`, `|ΔQ|/Q ≤ 3×10⁻¹²` after up to 13 crossings.
- RK45 (`10⁻⁸` and `10⁻¹⁰`): 18–21k steps/ray, 24–26 rays stalled at the step limit, winding number wrong for 4/20 rays already at `ε = 10⁻²` (~1 orbit) and 6/20 at `10⁻³`, fate wrong for 2/20 at `10⁻³`; exit-direction median error `1.8×10⁻²` at `ε = 10⁻²`. In the deterministic regime (`ε ≥ 10⁻³`) it is ~100× less accurate than the symplectic default at 20× the cost.
- Fixed on the way: after an exact landing on `rlim` the `u → r` round-off could leave `r` just below `rlim`, so the ray re-crossed the boundary every step until the step limit; the propagator now sets `r = rlim` and stops.
